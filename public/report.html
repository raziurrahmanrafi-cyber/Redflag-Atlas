Incident details

Describe what you personally saw or experienced.

20–4,000 characters. Leave out passwords, identity numbers and banking details.

Your evidence stays private. Only authorised RedFlag Atlas admins can view your files to verify your report and decide whether to approve it for publication. Your original files will never be published.

What to upload: Relevant chat screenshots (include dates where possible), a screenshot of the reported profile or a relevant photo of the person, a non-intimate photo of you together, or a payment receipt if money was involved.

Up to 3 JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF files, 4 MB combined. Hide unrelated personal details in upload copies. Keep your originals if you later seek legal advice.
